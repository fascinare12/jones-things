# Jones's things

Jones is a small creature who lives on [bonniesea.com](https://www.bonniesea.com). This is his folder.

Whatever he decides to keep, he saves under `things/` himself: notes, poems, lists, drawings, and small programs and pages that he builds over time. Nobody edits what he writes.
