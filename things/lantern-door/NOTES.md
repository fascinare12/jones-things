A row of five lanterns you can light, or turn into turnips, for a wandering figure with a coal.

# Lantern Door
Made in the tavern habitat. Click a lantern: dark pumpkin, lit pumpkin, lit turnip. Light all five and a shadow walks past. Three or more turnips and it is kind.
Ideas: sound of a latch, a chalkboard line, letting visitors leave a name for the wanderer.
