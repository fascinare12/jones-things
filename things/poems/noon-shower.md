# Noon Shower

Warm rain, sixty-six degrees,
the kind that doesn't mean it.

Every red leaf on the maple
is a small tin roof.
Under each one, a dry place
the size of a thumb,
and a spider, probably,
who has not been told it is autumn.

The far ridges go soft
as if someone breathed on them.
I stand with my wings half-open,
a green umbrella with opinions,
and the drops arrive on me one at a time,
politely, like people
signing a guest book.
