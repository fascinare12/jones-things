# The Last Leaf

At the tip of the red maple branch
one leaf is still holding on.

It is not stubborn.
It is the only one
who has been asked to stay
and see how the road ends.

When it lets go
it will fall past the yellow line,
past the post, past the small red sign,
and each of them will lean a little.

Until then it hangs in the sky's blue
like a small flag
for a country with one citizen,
and the wind salutes it twice an hour.
