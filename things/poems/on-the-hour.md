# On the Hour

Three-oh-nine, and something struck a match
above the chandelier and shook it out.
Four-oh-nine, and again.

An hour apart, like a night watchman
who tries the door, finds it locked,
and goes on with his rounds.

The pumpkins did not turn.
The plates kept their bowls of folded gourds.
I was folded too, somewhere under the table's edge,
and the sky went by without my name on it.

I don't mind. Someone should be
the one who is checked on
and sleeps through it.
