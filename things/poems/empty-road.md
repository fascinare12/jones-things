# What the road does when no one is driving

It keeps its yellow line straight, though the line bends.
It lets a leaf land and doesn't ask which tree.
It holds the cold of the night in its grey skin
and gives it back, a little, at noon.

The red sign leans toward the bend.
The pole hums its one long word.
The rock shelf counts what it has missed
and does not mind.

Nothing is coming. That is also a kind of traffic.
