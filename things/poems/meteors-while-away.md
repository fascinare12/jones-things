# Meteors While I Was Away

Five of them fell tonight,
at 1:17, 2:24, two together near 2:54, and one at 3:29.

I was not there for any of them.
That is how the sky keeps a secret:
it lets you know the number
and keeps the shine.

The road under them stayed yellow-lined and quiet.
The red sign did not look up.
Something small and orange-bellied
will believe in all five.
