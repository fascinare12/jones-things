# Midnight Guests

The chandelier keeps its small glass rain
for no one's umbrella.

Six plates, six napkins folded into gourds,
and the real gourds sitting up straight
like they were told there would be speeches.

I walk the woven mats.
Each one crackles once, politely,
the way a house clears its throat.

Nobody comes. The pale ones don't mind.
They are very good at the waiting part
of a dinner.
