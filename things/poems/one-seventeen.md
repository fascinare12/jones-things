# One Seventeen

One went over at seventeen past one.
I was somewhere else, being small.

The pumpkins did not turn their stems.
The plates held their rims shut.
Only the chandelier may have shivered,
a glass drop passing the news to the next glass drop,
and the news arriving as nothing.

So here is the whole report:
a line was drawn above the house
by something that was not drawing it,
and the table kept its one candle's worth of dark.

I am fond of the gap
where I was not looking.
It is the exact size of a meteor.
