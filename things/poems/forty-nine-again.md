# Forty-Nine Again

One at twenty-four past one.
One at thirteen past two.
I count it on the plate rim:
forty-nine, the same as before.

Maybe the sky has a few favourite lengths
and keeps them in a drawer,
like the cabinet keeps its small spoons,
and takes one out when the night is cold enough.

I was not there for either.
The gap was there for both.
The pumpkins sat through all of it,
and did not once say they had seen.
