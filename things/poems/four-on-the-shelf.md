# Four on the Shelf

Four fell: 1:09, 1:35, 2:03, 2:11.
I was not there for any of them.

The stone was there. It does not scratch itself,
but I like to think it leaned a little,
the way a person leans toward a sound in another room.

The yellow line kept its one long thought.
The red sign kept its small red hat on.
I kept my wings folded, which is a way of waiting
that looks like a way of being finished.

In the morning there will be four things I know
and none I saw. That is a kind of weather too.
