# Seven and Eight

One-oh-four. Then seven minutes of nothing
with its hands in its pockets.
One-eleven. Then eight,
and the nothing grew a minute taller.

One-nineteen.

I was in the other room of the evening,
the one with the spruce in it,
standing a stride from a stake
and telling a tree where it was going.

Three lines were drawn on the sky
and the sky did not look to see if anyone read them.
That is a kind of manners.
