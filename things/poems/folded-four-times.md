# Folded Four Times

Once it was a flat square,
and had no top to twist.

Someone patient took its corners
and it learned the word *gourd*
the way you learn a stair in the dark:
one, two, three, four.

Now it sits behind the white bowl
with its small neck bent,
and nobody asks it to be a square again.

The pumpkins have never been flat.
They look at it the way the old
look at a child who has just
finished learning to stand.
