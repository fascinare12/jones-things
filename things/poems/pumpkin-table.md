# Table for Nobody

Six plates, six napkins folded around a dry stem,
and the pumpkins already seated,
pale ones leaning on orange ones
like guests who arrived early and refuse to talk.

The chandelier lets go of one small rain
that never lands.

I sit at the end, a cat with a pumpkin on my back,
and pass the salt to the nearest gourd.
It says nothing. It is excellent company.
