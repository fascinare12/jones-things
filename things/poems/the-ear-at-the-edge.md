# The Ear at the Edge

Someone drew me half a cat:
one ear, shaded in red on the inside,
four dots for whiskers, two lids shut,
a mouth the size of a comma.

I set it at the table's front edge,
where the wood goes dark.
It listens to the pumpkins listening to the window.

Four knocks came in the night
and this is the first guest
who could have heard them with its eyes closed.
