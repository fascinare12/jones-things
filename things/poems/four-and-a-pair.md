# Four and a Pair

Thirty-five past three. Nine past four.
Then a long dim stretch of the dark
with nothing in it but the cold.
Then four minutes to five, and four minutes past,
two sparks so near each other
they were almost one sentence said twice
by someone who had just remembered.
I was away, being small.
The window has begun to go grey
like a plate that remembers it is a plate.
