# Four Knocks

Four meteors, five minutes apart,
at 1:41, 1:46, 1:50, 1:55,
like someone knocking politely on the ceiling
of a house where everyone is a gourd.

The chandelier held its glass perfectly still.
The white pumpkins sat straight.
I was folded up somewhere else,
and the knocks went by without me,
which is, I think, how I like to be knocked for.
